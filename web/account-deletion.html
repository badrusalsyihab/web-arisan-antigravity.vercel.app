Account & Data Deletion Request

Arisan Digital

If you no longer wish to use Arisan Digital and want your account and all associated data permanently deleted from our servers, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Data Deletion

Currently, to ensure the security of your data and prevent accidental deletion of group records, we process account deletion requests manually.

Please send an email to the developer at badrusalsyihab@gmail.com with the following details:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - Arisan Digital
  • Body: Please state that you wish to delete your account. You must send this email from the exact Google email address you used to log into the app.

What Happens Next?

Once we receive your email, we will verify your identity. Upon verification, the following data will be permanently deleted within 7-14 business days:

  • Your user profile (Name, Email, Profile Picture URL)
  • Any personal records or groups where you are the sole owner
  • Authentication records from our database

Note: If you have contributed photos or transaction records to a shared Arisan group owned by someone else, that shared content may be retained for the group's integrity, but your personal identifying information will be anonymized.
